Intergalactic Heist

Intergalactic Heist is a room-scale VR stealth and puzzle project where players infiltrate a high-security sci-fi facility, solve layered interaction challenges, and attempt extraction while managing risk. The gameplay includes 2 spawn points, a clue-based puzzle room, and a safe haptic room that lets the player steal either a Ray Gun or a Potion. After collecting the objective, the player must escape without getting caught; if caught by security, the player is sent to the death sequence and respawns to try again.

Project Description

Players begin by navigating a futuristic mission space and progressing through puzzle-controlled zones. To advance, they interact directly with world objects in VR: entering combinations, rotating locks under directional and speed constraints, and collecting required mission items. The flow includes two spawn points, a clue-based puzzle room, and a safe haptic room where the player can steal either a Ray Gun or a Potion. After obtaining an objective item, the player must escape without being caught; if security catches the player, the death sequence triggers and the player respawns to continue the heist attempt.
